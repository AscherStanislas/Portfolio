#### Workflow

### Game design — blood & execution

* Enemies bleed with every hit instead of dying; you execute them in the pool, whatever their HP
* Two health points: healing comes from executions, not from passive pickups
* Vampire power fantasy with constant tension around survival

### AI & combat readability

* Behaviors (states, NavMesh, visual scripting) tied to whether enemies are standing in the blood
* Enemies are threatening outside the pool and vulnerable inside it — readable without a text tutorial

### Level design — implicit tutorial

* First fight: three enemies already standing in blood when they spawn
* The "kill them in the blood" rule is learned through the situation, not through a cutscene or a pop-up

### Production

* Enemy design & behavior in visual scripting
* Level design of level 1 and contribution to level 3

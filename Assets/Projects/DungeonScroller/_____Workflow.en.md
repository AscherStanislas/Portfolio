#### Workflow

### Game design

- Building a dungeon-scroll mechanic for mobile: a single control that handles both attacking AND moving
- Simplified stats and RPG system: creating the equipment and stats system needed for long-term progression
- Designing reusable editors and dev methods: maximizing content while drastically cutting production costs

### Economy design

- Building an effective monetization strategy: diminishing farming returns, specialized offers, resource sinks
- Managing loot and its specifications to maximize retention
- Managing curves and balancing: an experience with as little friction as possible and a curve suited to grinding — satisfying, but able to be sped up through monetization

### Project management

- Managing a team of designers: prioritizing tasks, keeping the documentation clear and managing the creative strategy
- Communicating with the programming team and staying aligned with the game design brief
- Managing the overall schedule with a dedicated tool (HacknPlan)
